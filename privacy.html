Transparency & Respect

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 23, 2026

1. Overview

DotsPo ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to providing a mindful, relaxing, and privacy-focused gaming experience. We respect your personal privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains our data practices for the DotsPo mobile game and our website.

2. What Information We Collect

Offline Gameplay Data: All your progress through story chapters, unlocked milestones, high scores, and settings are stored locally on your device via local storage. We do not transmit or sell your individual gameplay actions.

Optional Online Features: If you choose to sign in with Google or Apple for cross-device cloud sync or global leaderboards, we receive minimal authenticated identity tokens and your chosen public display username. We do not access your contacts, personal files, or private profile information.

3. Advertisements & Tracking

DotsPo does not contain third-party ad networks, tracking pixels, or invasive behavioral profiling. We believe a mindful game should be completely free of disruptive banners and popups.

4. Sound & Audio Playback

Audio files (chimes, clicks, pops) are loaded directly from local application assets. No audio from your microphone or surroundings is ever accessed or recorded.

5. Children's Privacy

DotsPo is designed for audiences of all ages and does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this policy, please reach out to our team at jhuppoapps@gmail.com.
